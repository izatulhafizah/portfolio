About

A little about my academic journey

I am a lecturer in the Department of Geophysics and Meteorology at IPB University. I hold bachelor's and master's degrees in Astronomy from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB). My academic path brings together an interest in space and the Sun with questions about Earth's atmosphere and climate.

My work includes research, teaching, and scientific communication. I enjoy exploring data-driven approaches to atmospheric science and helping students connect physical concepts with observations and models.

Current interests: solar-terrestrial interactions, atmospheric radiation, rainfall variability, hydrometeorology, and remote sensing for solar resource assessment.

Rainfall and solar activity
project visual
Climate variability · 2026

Solar Cycle Influence on Rainfall Variability in Jabodetabek

Study area: Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, and Bekasi · Methods: time-series analysis and wavelet coherence

Investigating the relationship between sunspot numbers and rainfall variability in a tropical urban region using solar activity records and rainfall datasets. The project combines climate data analysis with time-frequency methods to examine possible associations across timescales.

Data: WAM-IPE · Variables: total electron content and neutral mass density
